# Prayer Point — Content Sheet

> A faith-based mobile app for prayer, reflection, and quiet time. Designed for everyone — from lifelong believers to the spiritually curious — Prayer Point helps you build a daily practice of stillness, gratitude, and connection.

---

## What is Prayer Point?

Prayer Point is a mobile app (iOS and Android) that turns prayer into a sustainable daily habit. It combines guided prayer, breathwork, voice journaling, and gentle accountability into one calming, distraction-free experience. The app is designed to feel like a quiet companion — not another notification stream.

**One-line pitch:** A pocket sanctuary for prayer, reflection, and peace of mind.

**Tagline:** *Your daily point of connection.*

---

## Who is it for?

- People who want to pray more consistently but struggle to remember or find the time
- Christians of any denomination looking for a private, ad-free prayer companion
- Anyone seeking a calmer mind — through breathwork, reflection, and intentional pauses from their phone
- Spiritually curious people who want a low-pressure way to explore prayer and stillness
- Busy parents, students, and professionals who want a few peaceful minutes built into their day

## Core Features

### 🙏 Quick Prayer
Guided prayer flows you can complete in under two minutes. Pick a category — gratitude, anxiety, family, healing, guidance — and the app walks you through a focused prayer with optional Bible verses.

### 🔥 Daily Streaks & Momentum
Build a real prayer habit. Track your day streak, longest streak, total prayers, and a 30-day "at peace" percentage that shows how consistent you've been over the last month — without punishing you for one off day.

### 🌬️ Breathwork
Box breathing and guided breath cycles to help you arrive in stillness before you pray. Helpful for anxiety and racing thoughts.

### 🎙️ Space (Voice Reflection Journal)
Tap the mic and just talk. Prayer Point transcribes your reflections automatically using AI, so you can journal hands-free during a walk, in the car, or before bed. Edit, save, and revisit anytime. Your reflections never leave your private library.

### 📖 Daily Bible Verse
A new verse each day, chosen for relevance and accessibility. No theological gatekeeping — just one meaningful line to carry with you.

### 📵 Phone Fast
A guided break from your phone. Set a timer (15 minutes to several hours) and let the app gently keep you accountable while you focus on prayer, family, work, or rest. Tracks your total minutes fasted from your phone.

### 🔔 Prayer Reminders
Customizable nudges that respect your quiet hours. Choose how often you want to be reminded and when not to be disturbed.

### 📊 Momentum Dashboard
Visualize your prayer life over time — streaks, weekly patterns, categories you pray about most, fast minutes accumulated, and reflections written.

### 👤 Private by Default
No accounts. No sign-up. No social features unless you want them. All data lives on your device.

## What makes Prayer Point different?

- **Universally inviting language.** "What's on your mind?" instead of religious jargon. The app meets people where they are.
- **Privacy-first architecture.** Your prayers, journal, and streaks live on your phone — not on a server. Voice reflections are transcribed in real time and the audio is never stored.
- **No social pressure.** No leaderboards, no follower counts, no shame if you skip a day.
- **Calming visual design.** Dark navy with warm gold accents. Inspired by quiet sanctuaries, not productivity apps.
- **Built to be put down.** The Phone Fast feature actively encourages users to spend less time on their phone — including in the app itself.

## Technical Overview (for developers, partners, and AI agents)

- **Platform:** iOS and Android (built with Expo and React Native)
- **Backend:** Lightweight Node.js API for voice transcription only
- **AI:** OpenAI Whisper-class model for voice-to-text transcription
- **Storage:** All user data stored locally on device (AsyncStorage). No user accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics tracking individuals.
- **Subscriptions:** Managed via RevenueCat → Apple App Store and Google Play Store
- **Open about its tools:** This app is built honestly using modern AI tooling but every prayer, reflection, and decision belongs entirely to the user

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Prayer Point only for Christians?
No. While it includes optional Bible verses and is rooted in a Christian sensibility, the core features — voice journaling, breathwork, Phone Fast, daily reflection — are meaningful to anyone seeking quiet time. The app uses universal language by default.

### Do I need to create an account?
No. Just download and start using. Your data stays on your phone.

### Are my prayers and reflections private?
Yes. Reflections are stored only on your device. Voice recordings are sent to a transcription service in real time and are not stored anywhere afterward.

### Does Prayer Point use AI?
Yes — only for one specific feature: turning your spoken reflections into text. The AI does not interpret, judge, or generate prayers for you. Your words are your own.

### How is this different from a meditation app?
Prayer Point is built around the act of prayer — speaking, listening, reflecting. Meditation apps focus on emptying the mind; Prayer Point focuses on connecting it. It also includes habit tracking, a daily verse, and Phone Fast — none of which exist in typical meditation apps.

### Will Prayer Point share or sell my data?
Never. There is no advertising network in the app, no third-party analytics tracking individuals, and no data sold to anyone.

### Can I export my reflections?
Export functionality is on the roadmap. For now, all reflections are stored on your device and can be viewed in the Space tab.

### What happens if I miss a day?
Your day streak resets, but your "at peace" percentage (a rolling 30-day measure of consistency) is forgiving — one missed day barely moves it. The app is designed to be sustainable, not punishing.
